## The challenge

Release information is often spread across planning documents, approvals, deployment notes, and team conversations. This makes it difficult to understand a release's current status or respond quickly when plans change.

## The approach

Built a centralized release management application for approvals, deployment planning, rollback tracking, versioning, and lifecycle history.

Automated Microsoft Teams release communication with mentions, threaded updates, approver feedback, CC visibility, and shareable links.
